# Missing Skills — Gap Analysis

**Context:** Personal skills deck (~131 skills). Role: executive consultant improving organizational delivery through **focus** and **organizing for flow**.

**What the deck already covers well (partial overlap, not gaps):** general reasoning, incentives and bias, bottlenecks / Theory of Constraints (single skill), capacity planning chain, Conway's Law, deep work and maker vs manager schedule, planning fallacy, exploration vs exploitation, change management and transformation chain, psychological safety and trust, Goodhart's Law, Cynefin, theory of change, stakeholder salience, mission command, OODA, startup discovery (Lean Startup, JTBD), portfolio and horizon thinking.

**What is thin or absent:** operational flow science, lean/kanban practice, delivery and DevOps metrics, portfolio prioritization economics, org design for flow, and consulting craft. The list below is ordered by relevance to your practice.

---

## Flow, queues, and throughput (core to “organizing for flow”)

| Skill | Why add it |
|-------|------------|
| **Little's Law** | Links WIP, cycle time, and throughput; foundational for any flow conversation with executives. |
| **WIP limits (pull systems)** | The primary lever for flow; bottlenecks alone do not teach explicit WIP policy. |
| **Flow efficiency vs resource efficiency** | Explains why “everyone busy” kills delivery; complements Efficiency and Bottlenecks. |
| **Lead time vs cycle time** | Shared vocabulary for commitments, SLAs, and predictability. |
| **Queueing / wait time** | Most delay is waiting, not working; extends Bottlenecks into queue dynamics. |
| **Batch size reduction** | Batch-and-queue is a common root cause of slow delivery. |
| **Cost of delay** | Economic lens for prioritization when capacity is scarce. |
| **WSJF (weighted shortest job first)** | Practical prioritization in product/portfolio settings. |
| **Kanban method** | End-to-end system for visualizing work, limiting WIP, and managing flow (beyond a single TOC card). |
| **Value stream mapping** | Diagnostic for end-to-end flow from customer need to delivery. |
| **Statistical variability / flow predictability** | Why percent-complete and deterministic dates fail; basis for probabilistic forecasting. |
| **Probabilistic forecasting (Monte Carlo)** | Executive-friendly alternative to single-date plans; pairs with Planning Fallacy. |
| **Service level expectations / flow commitments** | How teams commit without false precision. |
| **Drum–buffer–rope** | TOC operating pattern for synchronized flow (companion to Bottlenecks). |

## Delivery measurement and improvement

| Skill | Why add it |
|-------|------------|
| **DORA metrics** | Deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, MTTR — standard executive language for software delivery. |
| **Flow metrics (Flow Framework)** | Flow distribution, velocity, time, efficiency, load — portfolio-level flow health. |
| **Outcome vs output** | Separates shipped work from customer/ business impact; counters feature-factory thinking. |
| **Technical debt (as delivery constraint)** | Distinct from economic Debt; explains sustained slowdown and incident load. |
| **Reliability vs feature velocity trade-off** | Common executive tension; links to margin of safety and flow. |
| **Empirical process control (inspect and adapt)** | Underpins agile/lean improvement loops at team and program level. |
| **A3 / structured problem solving** | Concise narrative for operational improvement with executives. |
| **PDCA (Plan–Do–Check–Act)** | Lightweight continuous improvement cycle; complements post-mortem. |
| **Gemba (go and see)** | Ground truth for consultants; avoids map–territory errors in slide decks. |

## Summary

The deck is strong on **thinking**, **product discovery**, and **generic leadership**. For an executive consultant focused on **delivery, focus, and flow**, the largest gaps are:

1. **Flow science** (Little's Law, WIP, queues, cost of delay, forecasting).
2. **Measurement** (DORA, flow metrics, outcome vs output).
3. **Org design for flow** (Team Topologies, value streams, dependencies).
4. **Portfolio governance** (lean portfolio, funding, prioritization economics).
5. **Consulting craft** (hypothesis-driven work, executive synthesis, facilitation).

Adding 15–25 skills from the first four sections plus 2–3 pre-built chains would materially align the deck with your practice without diluting its mental-models character.
